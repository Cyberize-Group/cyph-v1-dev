# Welcome to your pilot workspace

Your AI helper is called **Vibe Cody**. It handles the technical work. You describe what you would like the app to do and tell it what you see.

Cyberize Engineering gives you access to the project. When you want to log in to a preview, they also give you a private settings file and a test login. To share your work, you need your own GitHub account with access to the project. Keep the settings file and password out of AI chat, screenshots and GitHub.

## Start here

1. Clone the project and open its folder in your AI tool. In Cursor, use **Clone Repository**, then open the folder. If you need help, Cyberize Engineering can give you instructions for your computer.
2. Start a new chat and say **Hi**.
3. At the workflow question, choose **1 — Guest frontend proposal**.
4. Vibe Cody suggests a name for your work area, called a branch. Say **yes**, or type a different name. Vibe Cody checks the name and sets up the work area.
5. Vibe Cody asks **"What would you like to change?"** Describe your idea in everyday words.

You do not need to prepare or paste anything. You also do not need to choose versions, run installation commands or repair a database. If your tool shows a permission prompt, Vibe Cody explains it in ordinary language.

## Seeing your change

When it is time to look at the app, Vibe Cody prepares it and gives you a browser address. It may first ask you to place the settings file. Put it in the project folder beside `package.json`, named exactly `.env.local` (not `.env.local.txt`). Then log in with your test login and tell Vibe Cody what you see.

The pilot may be shared with other guests. Use only the test account and settings supplied to you. If data looks real or unfamiliar, stop and tell Cyberize Engineering.

When you are finished, say **stop the app**. Vibe Cody stops what it started and confirms it. Closing the chat alone does not stop the app.

## Coming back

Open the same folder, start a new chat, say **Hi** and choose **1**. Vibe Cody finds your work area and continues where you left off. Your changes stay in place.

## Sharing your idea

When you are happy with a change, say **share my changes**. Vibe Cody asks you to confirm, because shared work is public on GitHub.

The first time, it signs you in to GitHub. It shows a short code and a link. Open the link, enter the code and approve. Then confirm that the GitHub name it shows is yours. The sign-in stays inside this project folder and does not change anything else on your computer.

Vibe Cody then saves your work, sends your branch to GitHub and opens a proposal (a pull request). It gives you the link. Cyberize Engineering reviews it, and only the Director merges. To share later changes, ask again; they are added to the same proposal. To sign out of this folder, say **sign me out of GitHub**.

## Getting help

If something fails, Vibe Cody gives you a short message to forward to **Cyberize Engineering**. You should not need to diagnose technical errors or choose a workaround.

This repository is public. Published code, branches and proposals are visible to other people. Never publish passwords, connection files or real patient or pharmacy information.
